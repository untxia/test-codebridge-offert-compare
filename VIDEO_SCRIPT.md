# 3-minute video script (screen recording; English, ~400 words)

**0:00 – 0:20 Problem.** "Buyers get a commercial offer, then a revised one, and compare them by hand. Offer Compare takes the two PDFs and lists what really changed, with the exact place in both documents."

**0:20 – 1:05 Normal case.** Click *normal case* → Compare. Point at: 5 changes (price 280→295, quantity 4→6, delivery moved, line removed, stated total). "The laptop was renamed and rows were reordered: shown under *Not reported as changes*." Click a source button → side-by-side viewer with highlights on both pages. "Notice the arithmetic check: the revised total says 19,060 but the lines sum to 18,960. I flag it, I don't fix it."

**1:05 – 1:30 Formatting only.** *formatting only* → "No changes." "Different fonts, `EUR 1 150,00`, written-out dates: not commercial changes."

**1:30 – 2:20 Ambiguous case.** *ambiguous case* → partial result. "One laptop line became two: split, upgrade, or new line? I don't guess — here's 560 € at stake." Click the answer → report recomputes. "The second question has zero amount: a rename or a swap?"

**2:20 – 2:40 Decline.** *scanned document* → "No text layer. I refuse rather than invent numbers."

**2:40 – 3:00 New input + honesty.** Upload a different PDF pair (the hold-out). "No LLM at runtime, ~0.15 s per pair, zero model cost. 0 missed or false changes on my sets, but I wrote those sets: real supplier PDFs are the open risk. Details in the notes."
